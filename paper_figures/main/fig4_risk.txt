SO-CVaR versus the average-year design (LP-Avg) on the out-of-sample test split. Each point is one climate x architecture x VoLL cell (30 total); x = LP-Avg, y = SO-CVaR, dashed line = equality, so points below favour SO-CVaR. Colour = VoLL level, marker = architecture. (a) Worst-fold unmet energy (a coarse tail proxy -- the largest of the 5 fold means; loss of load is essentially all thermal): SO-CVaR is lower in 30/30 cells (median 21% reduction). (b) Across-fold SD of total cost (year-to-year cost stability): SO-CVaR is lower in 30/30 cells (median 31% reduction). The tail gain in (a) is partly bought with ~1% more mean spend; the cost-stability gain in (b) is the cleaner, unconfounded result.
