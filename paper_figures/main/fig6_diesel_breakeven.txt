Diesel break-even analysis at the representative Med VoLL operating point (HVAC $3/kWh, critical $100/kWh; diesel is VoLL-independent). Costs are annualized USD/yr on test-split years. Diesel total cost is recomputed at any delivered price p as fixed_cost + gallons*p, where fixed_cost = total_cost - fuel_cost and gallons are price-independent. (a) For Marine (CA), the diesel cost line (grey dashed; thin grey = other climates) crosses the recommended SO-CVaR design of each architecture at 1.5-1.7 $/gal, far left of both the routine forward-delivery band ($13-45/gal, light) and the contested-delivery band ($100-600/gal, darker). (b) The break-even delivered diesel price for the recommended SO-CVaR design in every climate (bars: hatch = architecture; error bars = +/-1 SD across the 5 folds); because the three methods' costs differ by only ~1-3% (Fig 3), the break-even is nearly method-independent. It stays below ~9 $/gal -- below even the routine forward-delivery band ($13-45/gal) in every climate, and 11x-766x under the contested band -- so the renewable microgrid is cheaper than a genset at any realistic forward-operating-base fuel price, without relying on the contested premium.
