Diesel break-even analysis at the representative Med VoLL operating point (HVAC $3/kWh, critical $300/kWh; diesel is VoLL-independent). Costs are annualized USD/yr on test-split years. Diesel total cost is recomputed at any delivered price p as fixed_cost + gallons*p, where fixed_cost = total_cost - fuel_cost and gallons are price-independent. (a) For Marine (CA), the diesel cost line (grey dashed; thin grey = other climates) crosses the best renewable design of each architecture (min mean test total cost across methods) at 2.7-2.8 $/gal, far left of the plausible $100-600/gal delivered-fuel band (shaded). (b) The break-even delivered diesel price for the best renewable design in every climate (bars: colour = winning method, hatch = architecture; error bars = +/-1 SD across the 5 folds) stays below ~10 $/gal, an order of magnitude under the shaded resupply band, so the renewable microgrid is cheaper than a genset at any realistic forward-operating-base fuel price.
