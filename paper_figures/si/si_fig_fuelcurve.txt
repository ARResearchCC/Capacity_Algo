Diesel generator fuel curve used in the model. Hourly fuel use follows the standard load-dependent linear (HOMER/NREL) form, fuel rate = F0 x GenSize + F1 x P_out (gal/h), shown normalised per kW of rated capacity against the load fraction (output / rated capacity). The no-load (idle) intercept F0 = 0.0215 gal/h per kW rated is consumed whenever the generator runs; the marginal slope F1 = 0.065 gal/h per kW is the extra fuel per kW of output, reaching 0.086 gal/h per kW at full load. The nonzero intercept encodes part-load inefficiency, so a lightly loaded generator burns more fuel per kWh delivered than a fully loaded one.
