VoLL sensitivity of the FOB microgrid in a representative marine climate (California; pooling across climates is dominated by Alaska). Lines show fold-mean out-of-sample (test-split) results versus the value-of-lost-load level (Low: HVAC $1/Critical $30; Med: $3/$100; High: $10/$300 per kWh); colour = method, marker fill and line style = architecture (open/dashed = PV+battery, filled/solid = with PCM). (a) Annualized total cost (USD/yr); (b) total unmet load (kWh/yr). Bands are +/-1 SD across the 5 folds. Method cost differences are small and within the fold bands at Low and Med VoLL, separating only at High. With PCM at High VoLL, SO-CVaR is the cheapest method (-51 $/yr vs LP-Avg, -142 $/yr vs LP-Worst), while its unmet load tracks the robust LP-Worst (below LP-Avg, slightly above LP-Worst). Without PCM this cost crossover does not occur (SO-CVaR remains a small premium over LP-Avg).
