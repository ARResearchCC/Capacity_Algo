VoLL sensitivity of the FOB microgrid in a representative marine climate (California; pooling across climates is dominated by Alaska). Lines show fold-mean out-of-sample (test-split) results versus the value-of-lost-load level (Low: HVAC $1/Critical $100; Med: $3/$300; High: $10/$600 per kWh); colour = method, marker fill and line style = architecture (open/dashed = PV+battery, filled/solid = with PCM). (a) Annualized total cost (USD/yr); (b) total unmet load (kWh/yr). Bands are +/-1 SD across the 5 folds. As VoLL rises, the SO-CVaR cost advantage over LP-Avg and LP-Worst GROWS: for PCM in California the SO-CVaR-vs-LP gap moves from roughly break-even at Low to ~$100-180/yr cheaper at High, while its unmet load stays at or below the LP methods.
